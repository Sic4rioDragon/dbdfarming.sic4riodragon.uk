Carry farming

Needs testing
Could progress: Bodyguard · Heavy Burden · Multi-tasker · Cutting Out
Killer recommended: Mad Grit
Killer optional: Agitation · Iron Grasp
Survivor healing: Circle of Healing · Botany Knowledge · Leader
Test idea
  1. Down Survivor A and pick them up.
  2. Survivors B, C and D rotate taking protection hits while A is being carried.
  3. Use the healing perks and boon to reset everyone quickly between hits.
  4. Those hits should progress Heavy Burden for the killer and Bodyguard for survivors.
  5. Let injured survivors get downed while A is still being carried for Multi-tasker.
  6. Mad Grit should help keep A's wiggle progress under control while the loop is running.
  7. When ready, stop hitting survivors and let A wiggle free for Cutting Out.
Main thing to test: how reliably the protection hits register and how many healing perks we actually need.

Controlled chase loop

Needs testing
Could progress: A Worthy Opponent · Leapfrog · Whiffing to Success · Close Shave · Desperate Escape · With Scars to Show · Insult and Injury · Hiding Seeker
Useful survivor perk: Any Means Necessary
Test idea
  1. Start a controlled chase around shack or another strong window/pallet area.
  2. Keep chase going for at least 90 seconds.
  3. Have the killer deliberately miss attacks before vaults.
  4. Vault repeatedly while chase is still active.
  5. Take a hit and rushed-vault shortly afterwards.
  6. Pallet stun the killer and then lose chase.
  7. Reset pallets with Any Means Necessary where possible.
  8. Try ending some chases by entering a locker for Hiding Seeker.

Path to Salvation stack

Needs testing
Could stack: Path to Salvation · The Show Must Go On · Under Pressure · Sorted

The Path to Salvation method itself is already confirmed. What needs testing is how cleanly the other injured-survivor achievements can be stacked into the same loop.

Test idea
  1. Stay injured for most of the match.
  2. Repair generators while injured.
  3. Try to hit the Great Skill Checks needed for Under Pressure.
  4. Pre-heal to around 90% before the generator finishes.
  5. Finish the heal directly after the generator pops.
  6. Escape while having spent over half the match injured.

Hatch triple

Needs testing
Could stack: Outrun Evil · Backdoor Escape · Agonising Escape
Test idea
  1. Let three survivors leave or die.
  2. Find the hatch with the final survivor.
  3. Killer downs that survivor beside the open hatch.
  4. The survivor crawls through the hatch while in the Dying State.

All three requirements overlap enough that this may give all three at once. We just need to confirm it.

Hook / unhook loop

Needs testing
Could progress: Bloodied Hands · Fleet of Foot · One More Step · Taking One for the Team
Possible perks: Babysitter · No Mither · Shoulder the Burden
Test idea
  1. The achievement hunter stays Broken.
  2. They safely unhook survivors for Bloodied Hands.
  3. Babysitter gives movement speed for Fleet of Foot.
  4. The unhooked survivor intentionally takes a hit while Endurance is active for One More Step.
  5. Another survivor takes protection hits near the hook for Taking One for the Team.
  6. Use Shoulder the Burden where useful to extend the hook rotation.

Skilled Huntress + Close Shave

Needs testing

Use the already-confirmed Skilled Huntress boon-distance setup.

Before landing the real hatchet, Huntress deliberately throws some close misses around the survivor. If those count as dodged projectiles, the survivor could work on Close Shave at the same time.

Main test: how close a hatchet has to pass before Close Shave actually registers it.

Cripple Them All + Cutting Out

Needs testing

Use the confirmed two-trap Trapper loop.

Instead of immediately resetting every pickup, occasionally stop the trap loop and let the carried survivor wiggle free for Cutting Out.

It should work fine, but we need to check whether it is actually faster than doing Cutting Out separately.

Pallet rescue loop

Needs testing
Main achievement: Rescue Mission
Recommended: Any Means Necessary
Test idea
  1. Down a survivor beside a pallet.
  2. Killer picks them up.
  3. Achievement hunter pallet-stuns the killer and gets the save.
  4. Reset the pallet with Any Means Necessary.
  5. Repeat.

Endgame cleanup

Needs testing
Could progress: Ever Vigilant · Final Days · No One Left Behind · Escaping the Nightmare · Break For It
Test idea
  1. Finish the normal farming method before leaving.
  2. Power the exit gates.
  3. Open one gate to start Endgame Collapse.
  4. Do any remaining healing needed for Ever Vigilant.
  5. Take protection hits during EGC for Final Days.
  6. Hook and rescue somebody after the gates are powered for No One Left Behind.
  7. Anybody working on Break For It leaves while Broken.

If this flows cleanly, it could just become a short achievement cleanup at the end of a normal farm.

Judgment achievement round

Needs testing
Could stack: Sacrilege · Sacrosanct · Above Judgment · Under my Dominion
Test idea
  1. Survivors deliberately taunt Judgment to gain Heresy for Sacrilege.
  2. Judgment downs and Exiles one of those survivors for Sacrosanct.
  3. The survivor escapes Exile without being hit by Seeds of Punishment for Above Judgment.
  4. Set up long-range Divine Light hits from at least 16 metres for Under my Dominion.

Witness to Heresy is already confirmed separately, so if this works there could eventually be one bigger Judgment farming session covering nearly everything.

Grab achievement match

Needs testing
Could work on: Silent Approach · Quick Draw · Taste the Darkness · From the Deep
Test idea

These are all grab/interrupt achievements, so a group could dedicate one match to rotating through the required interactions:

  • Generator grabs for Silent Approach.
  • Totem grabs for Quick Draw.
  • Vault grabs for Taste the Darkness.
  • Locker grabs for From the Deep.

Basement healing

Needs testing
Main achievement: Fearless Healer
Useful perks: Botany Knowledge · Leader · other healing speed perks

Have the killer repeatedly injure survivors in basement, heal the health states there, and repeat.

Could potentially be combined with some of the other healing achievements too.
