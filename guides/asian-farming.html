Asian Farming

Easy to solo queue into when it works, but slower and much less reliable than organized farming.

Hens showcaseOrganized farmingChange region

Why people do it

  • Easy to solo queue.
  • Very relaxed when it works.
  • No need to round up a full group first.
  • Good broad scoring progress, so I also recommend it for Survivor grades for now.

Main downsides

  • Usually slower than organized farming.
  • Can go wrong more easily.
  • Can include players who do not know what is happening or do not want to participate.
  • Can expose you to griefers or cheaters.

Important

Asian farming can be risky under BHVR's rules because it is unorganized and can involve unwilling participants. The important part is that everybody in the lobby understands what is happening and accepts the farming. Organized farming avoids that problem because the group is arranged ahead of time.

Offerings

Bloody Party Streamers or anniversary cakes are basically required in practice. If somebody does not bring one, people may crash the server or shun/kill that player instead of farming with them.

What perks are normally used?

The rather essential setup is Reactive Healing, Self-Care and Any Means Necessary. Your partner can substitute the Any Means for you, and technically every missing perk can be replaced, but missing the core pieces slows the game down hard and is generally unwanted.

The fourth perk can be whatever helps the loop: Leader, Prove Thyself or Deja Vu are common choices.

How long does a round take?

A proper Asian farming round is usually around 10 minutes and, if done correctly, you normally max everything including XP.

The Hens video is best treated as a showcase of the base idea now. The core actions are still the same, but current farming is generally faster and more optimized.
