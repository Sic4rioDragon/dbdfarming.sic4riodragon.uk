Asian Farming

Easy to solo queue into when it works, but slower and much less reliable than organized farming.

Hens showcaseOrganized farmingHome

Why people do it

  • Easy to solo queue.
  • Very relaxed when it works.
  • No need to round up a full group first.

Main downsides

  • Usually slower.
  • Can go wrong more easily.
  • Can include players who do not know what is happening or do not want to farm.
  • Can expose you to griefers or cheaters.

Important note

Asia farming can violate BHVR's Terms of Use due to its unorganized nature and the risk of including unwilling participants. If everybody in the lobby clearly accepts the farm, that is the important part.

What is usually expected

Bloody Party Streamers or anniversary cakes are basically required. If you do not bring one, people may crash the server or simply shun and kill you.

The rather essential perks are Reactive Healing, Self-Care and Any Means Necessary. Your partner can substitute Any Means for you, and technically any missing perk can be replaced, but it slows the game down hard and is generally unwanted.

The fourth perk can be whatever helps: commonly Leader, Prove Thyself or Deja Vu.

How long is a round?

Usually around 10 minutes. If done correctly you normally max everything, including XP. Hens' video is more of a showcase of the base idea now; the core actions are still the same, but current farming is generally faster and more optimised.
