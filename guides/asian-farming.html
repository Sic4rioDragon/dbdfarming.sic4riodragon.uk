Asian Farming

Easy to get into without arranging a full group first. When the lobby knows the loop it is relaxed, consistent and can give 200k+ Bloodpoints in a roughly 10-minute round.

Why people do it

  • Easy to solo queue into when you find the right lobbies.
  • Very relaxed once everybody understands the loop.
  • No need to round up a full five-person group first.
  • Very good all-round scoring, XP and Survivor grade progress.

Main downsides

  • Slower than the fastest organized methods.
  • Can go wrong when somebody does not understand the farm.
  • Random players may not want to participate.
  • You can also run into griefers or cheaters.

Important

Asian farming is unorganized, so there is always a chance of involving somebody who never agreed to farm. If somebody clearly does not want to participate, do not force the match around them. Organized farming avoids that problem because the whole group is arranged ahead of time.

Quick round checklist

BPS / anniversary cake Meet in the middle 2 hits each + full heals Split into 2 duos Gens ↔ pallets/saves Finish all 5 gens Meet Killer at gate Trade hooks Escape

Offerings

Bloody Party Streamers or anniversary cakes are basically required in practice. Showing up without one is a good way to get shunned/killed instead of included in the farm, and some lobbies will simply crash rather than continue.

How a round works

If everybody does their part, following the basic actions from the Asian farming showcase is enough for a good round. A properly run match is usually around 10 minutes and can give 200k+ Bloodpoints per Survivor.

  1. Everybody meets around the middle of the map.
  2. The Killer hits each Survivor twice. Survivors fully heal after every hit.
  3. Split into two duos.
  4. One duo works on 1-2 generators together while the other duo handles pallet stuns and saves.
  5. Switch jobs so the generator duo moves to pallets/saves and the other duo starts doing generators.
  6. Across the five generators, each Survivor should average participation in about 2.5 generators. The duos need to work together on the gens to get the full point spread.
  7. Once all five generators are done, everybody meets the Killer at the exit gate.
  8. The first duo trades hooks twice, then the second duo does the same.
  9. Once a Survivor has finished their part, they can escape.

Who does what?

Duo A

Starts on generators together, then switches to pallet stuns and saves.

Duo B

Starts on pallet stuns and saves, then switches to generators together.

Killer

Runs the hit/heal phase, works through the pallet interactions and finishes with the hook trades at the gate.

What usually goes wrong?

  • Somebody does not bring the expected offering.
  • A player does not know the farming loop or simply does not want to farm.
  • People repair generators alone instead of with their duo.
  • Survivors do not fully heal during the opening hit/heal phase.
  • The Killer kills somebody before their part is finished.
  • Someone leaves before the hook trades are done.
  • The region/lobby is just too quiet at that time.
