DBD Bloodpoint Farming

The farming styles on this site are built around filling the scoring categories quickly instead of just stretching a normal match out forever.

What actually gives Bloodpoints?

Both roles earn Bloodpoints across four scoring categories. That is why a good farm deliberately rotates through different actions instead of only doing Generators or only trading hits.

Killer scoring categories

Brutality

Hits, breaking pallets/walls and other direct combat actions.

Deviousness

Using the Killer's power and getting value from power-specific actions.

Hunter

Finding Survivors, starting and maintaining chases, and related tracking actions.

Sacrifice

Hooks, sacrifices and other sacrifice-category actions.

Survivor scoring categories

Objectives

Generator work, exit gates and other objective actions.

Survival

Staying alive, escaping chases and getting out of the Trial.

Altruism

Healing, rescues, protection hits and helping the team.

Boldness

Chases, stuns, risky Killer-proximity actions and similar interactions.

Recommended

Organized farming

Five people queue together and use known methods. The group can deliberately fill categories, reset heals quickly and control match length without guessing whether random players want to participate.

A strong organized round can be only a few minutes depending on the method; the public spreadsheet has builds sorted by BP/hour, BP/offering and difficulty.

Asian farming

Much easier to enter solo and usually around ten minutes when it goes correctly. It is slower and less reliable, but it normally gives very broad scoring progress and is also useful for Survivor grades.

If you are just playing normal matches

Outside an organized farm, Killer usually has more direct control over how many scoring actions happen because the Killer controls chases, power use, hooks and a lot of the match pacing. Survivor BP is more dependent on teammates, the Killer and whether you survive long enough to touch several categories.

Killer

  • Use your power regularly instead of ignoring it.
  • Rotate between Survivors instead of spending the whole match on one chase.
  • Kick/break things when it makes sense so Brutality is not left behind.
  • Spread hooks around if your goal is BP rather than ending the match immediately.

Survivor

  • Do Generator work, but do not ignore heals and rescues.
  • Take safe chase/Boldness opportunities instead of hiding the entire match.
  • Protection hits and team heals help fill Altruism.
  • Escaping matters, but a match where you touched several categories is usually more useful than one where you only sat on Generators.

Offerings

For the farming styles documented here, Bloody Party Streamers or anniversary cakes are the normal expectation. In Asian farming they are basically required in practice. Organized groups can accept other BP offerings if everybody agrees first, but they are generally still unwanted.
