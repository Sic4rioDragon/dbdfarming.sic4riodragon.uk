Organized Farming

The farming style I recommend. Everybody knows the plan, queues together and agrees to the farm before the match.

Join the DiscordAchievement huntingHome

Why organized farming

  • Everybody knows the plan before the match.
  • No random unwilling players are involved.
  • It is much more efficient than random lobby farming.
  • It is the best way I know for bloodpoints and also works really well for grades and achievements.

What you usually need

  • Bloody Party Streamers or anniversary cakes are generally expected.
  • Other bloodpoint offerings can be accepted if it is communicated first and everyone is fine with it, but they are still usually unwanted.
  • Reactive Healing and Self-Care are very common.
  • The exact build depends on the method the group is running.

How it works

All 5 players get into VC, queue together and follow one of the known builds/methods. A good round is usually around 10 minutes and can max everything including XP.

How do I find my group?

The easiest place is the Bloodpoint Farming Discord. It is the largest and most active farming community out there. Between events it can be more barren, but anyone is welcome to make a group and there is usually at least one group online per day on normal non-event days too.
