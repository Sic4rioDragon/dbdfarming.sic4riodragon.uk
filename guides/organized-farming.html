Organized Farming

The farming style I recommend. Everybody knows the plan, queues together and agrees to the farm before the match.

Join the DiscordFarming methodsAchievement hunting

Why organized farming?

  • Everybody knows the plan before the match.
  • No random unwilling players are involved.
  • The group can use much more efficient builds and methods.
  • It works for Bloodpoints, XP, grades and achievement hunting.

What do I need?

  • Bloody Party Streamers or anniversary cakes are generally expected.
  • Other Bloodpoint offerings can be accepted if it is communicated first and everybody is fine with it, but they are still usually unwanted.
  • Reactive Healing and Self-Care are very common in the farm builds.
  • The exact perks depend on which method the group is running.

How does organized DBD farming work?

All 5 players get into VC, queue together and follow one of the known builds/methods. Because all five people are cooperating, the group can rotate hits, heals, chases and objectives without wasting time figuring out whether the other side is farming.

The farming spreadsheet has several methods with different trade-offs between BP/hour, BP/offering, group size and difficulty.

How do I find a farming group?

The easiest place is the Bloodpoint Farming Discord. It is the largest and most active farming community I know for this. Between events it can be more barren, but anyone is welcome to make a group and there is usually at least one group online per day on normal non-event days too.

Achievements too

The same setup is great for achievement hunting because everybody can agree on the extra setup before queueing. The achievement section has optimized methods for things like Triggered, Bless You, Skilled Huntress, Dream Demon and carry/protection-hit achievements.
