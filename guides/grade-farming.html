Killer grade method

This is currently the fastest and best Killer grading method I know.

Pyramid Head setup

  • Use Pyramid Head.
  • Get 5 M2s while the Survivors maintain Chase, commonly around basement.
  • Healing side can use Reactive Healing, Self-Care, Desperate Measures and Leader on one Survivor.
  • Then 2 Corrective Action while they keep Chase going during heals.
  • Final down with M2, then cage everyone.
