Grade Farming

The dedicated method I have right now is for Killer grades. Survivor grades are better handled through Asian farming for now.

Killer grade method

The fastest and best Killer grading method I know right now uses Pyramid Head with Survivors maintaining chase while repeatedly healing and taking M2 hits.

Pyramid Head setup

  1. Use Pyramid Head and keep the Survivors in a controlled chase, commonly around basement.
  2. Get 5 M2 hits while the Survivors maintain chase.
  3. For the healing side, use Reactive Healing and Self-Care; one Survivor can bring Leader, with Desperate Measures / Corrective Action used in the setup as needed.
  4. Keep the chase going while the Survivors heal/reset.
  5. After the repeated M2/heal sequence, down with M2 and cage everyone to finish.

Survivor grades

I do not have a dedicated Survivor grade method here yet. For now I recommend Asian farming, because when the round goes correctly you normally get strong progress across all the Survivor scoring categories.
