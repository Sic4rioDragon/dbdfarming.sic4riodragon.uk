How does farming work?

Farming is basically playing the match in a controlled way so everybody gets what they need instead of playing it like a normal match. Depending on the method that can mean repeated heals, Chases, downs, hooks, cages, pallets and other actions.

What is the difference between organized and Asian farming?

Organized farming is planned: everyone knows what is happening and is usually in VC. Asian farming is easier to solo queue into, but it is slower, less reliable and can involve unwilling players.

How do I find a group?

Best place is the Bloodpoint Farming Discord.

How can I change my region?

Most people use a region changer. The best known option for this is Make Your Choice, usually shortened to MYC.

Make Your Choice (MYC)

What offerings do I need?

Bloody Party Streamers or anniversary cakes. In Asian farming they are basically required. In organized farming other bloodpoint offerings can be accepted if it is communicated and everyone is okay with it, but they are still usually unwanted.

Can achievements be done in custom games?

No. DBD achievements need public matches, which is why organized farming works so well for achievement hunting.

Can I do achievements while farming?

Yes. Quite a few are much easier with a cooperating group. Tell the group what you need before the match and use the achievement guide or method list.
