Achievement Hunting

Tested ways to get some of the more annoying DBD achievements done in organized farming matches.

About these methods

Everything on this page is something me and my friend have actually tested. Some of the methods are meant to finish one annoying achievement as fast as possible, while others can just be fitted into a normal farming match.

Achievements have to be done in public matches, so these are meant for organized farming groups that queue into each other rather than custom games.

Survivor achievements

Not Today

Tested

Unhook yourself.

Method: Have the killer facecamp you and let the anti-camp meter fill.

Once you can unhook yourself, do it and repeat whenever possible.

Shoulder the Burden does not actually help with this achievement.

Fleet of Foot

Tested

Give other survivors movement speed boosts.

Recommended: Babysitter
Also works: Boon: Dark Theory
Optional: Shoulder the Burden on the person doing the achievement
Dark Theory method

Set up Dark Theory and have the other survivors repeatedly walk in and out of the boon.

Do not walk back into the boon until the previous speed boost is fully gone.

Dark Theory can be inconsistent with tracking, so even though the achievement wants 50 boosts you can end up doing closer to 100.

Babysitter is generally the easier option. Shoulder the Burden can also give the achievement hunter another useful unhook during the match.

Path to Salvation

Tested

Gain a health state shortly after a generator is completed.

Method
  1. Let the killer hit you.
  2. Self-Care or let somebody heal you to at least around 90%.
  3. Finish a generator.
  4. Have one or two survivors immediately finish your heal.
  5. Repeat for the next generator.

The achievement only needs this a few times, so an organized group can get it done very quickly.

Bless You

Tested

Bless Hex Totems repeatedly without waiting for random killers to bring Hex perks.

Killer: Hex: Two Can Play
Survivor 1: Any boon perk
Survivor 2: Any Means Necessary
Full loop
  1. Cleanse every totem except one dull totem next to a pallet.
  2. Stun the killer once.
  3. The survivor doing Bless You starts blessing the remaining dull totem.
  4. At around 80% blessing progress, stun the killer again.
  5. Two Can Play activates on the dull totem while it is being blessed.
  6. Finish the blessing so it counts as blessing a Hex.
  7. The killer snuffs the boon.
  8. Use Any Means Necessary to reset the pallet.
  9. Repeat.
Do not forget the killer has to snuff the boon after each completed blessing.

Survivor Adepts

Tested

Bring only the survivor's three unique perks like normal.

Then either finish all generators and leave, or have the killer 3K and let the Adept survivor take hatch.

Tell the group before the match since the Adept player cannot bring the usual farming build.

Killer achievements

Witness to Heresy

Tested

See the Obsession's aura for a total of 300 seconds.

Build: Celestial Witness · Lethal Pursuer · Friends 'Til the End · Nemesis

This build makes it much easier to keep getting useful Obsession aura reads instead of trying to slowly build the achievement through normal games.

Dream Demon

Tested

Pull survivors into The Nightmare's Dream World repeatedly.

Survivor perk: Deadline
Method
  1. Go to the shack generator.
  2. Set the survivors up together around the generator.
  3. The survivor closest to the pallet can stay seated.
  4. The other survivors move toward that survivor to get pulled into the Dream World.
  5. The Nightmare keeps spawning fake pallets and triggering them as soon as the survivors are out of the Dream World.
  6. Repeat the loop.

Cripple Them All

Tested

Pick survivors directly out of Trapper's Bear Traps.

Add-ons: Fastening Tools · Trapper Gloves
Main method
  1. You only need one survivor and at least two traps.
  2. Set both traps.
  3. The survivor walks into one trap.
  4. Pick them directly out of it.
  5. While carrying them, walk into the other open trap.
  6. Always keep one open trap ready for yourself.
  7. Reset and repeat.
Alternative setup

You can gamble with Iridescent Stone and Trapper Sack instead.

If you do that, having around three or four traps on the floor is better so you have more chances of one opening for the next loop.

Skilled Huntress

Tested

Down survivors with hatchets from at least 24 metres away.

Survivor: Bring a boon, Circle of Healing works fine.
Method
  1. Set up the boon.
  2. The boon radius is 24 metres, so use its edge as your distance marker.
  3. Survivors stand around the outside edge of the boon.
  4. Huntress stands just outside the required distance and throws hatchets at them.
  5. Standing beside a locker makes hatchet refills much faster.
  6. Heal and repeat.

Killer Adepts

Tested

Use only that killer's three unique perks like normal.

If you just want the Adept done quickly, have all four survivors die in basement.

Add-ons are fine. It is the perk restriction that matters for the Adept.
