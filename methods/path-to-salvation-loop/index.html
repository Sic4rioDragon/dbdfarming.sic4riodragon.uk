Path to Salvation gen/heal loop

Pre-heal, pop the Generator, then finish the heal immediately.

Path to Salvation

How to do it

  1. Take a hit.
  2. Self-Care or have someone heal you to around 90% or more.
  3. Finish a Generator.
  4. Have one or two Survivors immediately finish your heal.
  5. Repeat on the next Generator.

Notes

  • This can be worked into a normal organised farm without much extra time.
