Hatch crawl stack

Down the last Survivor beside an open Hatch and have them crawl through it.

Outrun EvilBackdoor EscapeAgonizing Escape

How to do it

  1. Let the other three Survivors leave or die.
  2. Find/open the Hatch for the final Survivor.
  3. Killer downs that Survivor right beside it.
  4. Survivor crawls through the Hatch while in Dying State.

Options / variants

Triple count
The requirements overlap enough that this may award Outrun Evil, Backdoor Escape and Agonizing Escape together. Check platform tracking before calling it guaranteed.
