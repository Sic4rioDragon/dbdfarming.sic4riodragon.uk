Hag two-trap teleport farm

Rotate between two Phantasm Traps so one Survivor can trigger them almost nonstop.

TriggeredEven Punishment

How to do it

  1. Place Trap A and Trap B close enough to rotate between.
  2. The Survivor triggers A and then moves into B.
  3. Teleport to A and replace it.
  4. Teleport to B and replace it.
  5. The Survivor loops back into A and keeps alternating.
  6. Repeat until Triggered is done.

Notes

  • For Even Punishment, first have each of the four Survivors trigger a trap and take one hit after the teleport. Then one Survivor can stay for the long Triggered grind.
