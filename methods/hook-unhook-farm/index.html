Hook / unhook achievement farm

Rotate hooks, safe unhooks and protection hits so several hook achievements move together.

Bloodied HandsFleet of FootOne More StepTaking One For The Team

Survivor perks / setup

BabysitterNo Mither / another Broken setup for Bloodied HandsShoulder the Burden (optional)Reactive Healing on the rest of the teamMettle of Man for Survivors farming repeated Protection Hits

How to do it

  1. Achievement hunter stays Broken when working on Bloodied Hands.
  2. Safely unhook Survivors.
  3. Babysitter gives the Haste for Fleet of Foot.
  4. The freshly unhooked Survivor can intentionally take a hit while Endurance is active for One More Step.
  5. Another Survivor takes protection hits around the unhooked player for Taking One For The Team.
  6. Reset and rotate hooks.

Notes

  • Shoulder the Burden can help extend the rotation but is not required for the achievements themselves.
  • Mettle of Man is useful on whoever is taking the protection hits. After the third Protection Hit it can absorb the next hit that would normally down them, which gives the group another protection-hit opportunity before resetting.
