Injured Generator farm

Stay injured while working Generators, farm Great Skill Checks, then use the Path to Salvation heal timing.

Path to SalvationUnder PressureSortedThe Show Must Go On

Survivor perks / setup

DeadlineReactive Healing

How to do it

  1. Get injured early and stay injured for most of the Trial.
  2. Repair Generators while injured and hit Great Skill Checks for Under Pressure.
  3. Before a Generator finishes, pre-heal to around 90% if you are doing Path to Salvation.
  4. Finish the Generator, then immediately finish the heal.
  5. Get injured again and continue until the match is done.
  6. Escape after spending more than half the Trial injured for Sorted.

Notes

  • The Path to Salvation part is already used; the bigger stack is where you can save extra matches.
