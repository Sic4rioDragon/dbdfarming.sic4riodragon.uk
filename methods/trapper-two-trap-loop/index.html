Trapper two-trap loop

One Survivor and two traps are enough to repeatedly get direct trap pickups.

Cripple Them AllCutting Out

Killer add-ons

Fastening ToolsTrapper Gloves

How to do it

  1. Set two Bear Traps.
  2. The Survivor walks into one trap.
  3. Pick them directly out of the trap.
  4. While carrying them, walk into the second open trap so the Survivor gets dropped/free.
  5. Reset and repeat, always keeping one open trap ready for yourself.

Notes

  • Do not hit the trapped Survivor; the achievement progress comes from grabbing them directly out of the trap.

Options / variants

Iridescent Stone + Trapper Sack
You can gamble on traps reopening themselves. Put 3-4 traps on the floor so there is a better chance one is ready for the next loop.
Cutting Out add-on
If a Survivor still needs Cutting Out, occasionally skip the self-trap drop and let them wiggle free instead.
