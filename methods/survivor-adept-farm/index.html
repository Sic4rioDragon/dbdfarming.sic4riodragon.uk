Survivor Adept farm

Use the required three unique perks, then either finish the game normally or make the Adept player the hatch escape.

Survivor Adepts

How to do it

  1. The Adept player brings only that Survivor's three Unique Perks.
  2. Either complete all Generators and let them escape, or have the Killer 3K and give that Survivor hatch.

Notes

  • Tell the group first because the Adept player cannot use the usual farming loadout.
