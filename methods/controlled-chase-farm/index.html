Controlled chase farm

Use shack or another clean pallet/window area to farm a whole block of chase achievements.

Survivor perks / setup

Any Means Necessary

How to do it

  1. Start one long controlled Chase.
  2. Keep it going for at least 90 seconds.
  3. Killer deliberately misses attacks before vaults.
  4. Survivor repeats Pallet and Window vaults while the Chase is active.
  5. Take a hit and Rushed Vault within 3 seconds when working on Desperate Escape.
  6. Pallet stun the Killer, then break Chase for Insult and Injury / With Scars to Show.
  7. Reset useful Pallets with Any Means Necessary.
  8. For Hiding Seeker, end selected Chases by entering a Locker and having the Killer leave.

Options / variants

Close Shave counting
Use close Basic Attack misses first. Projectile misses can also be tried, but check how generously they register.
