Grab achievement match

Use one dedicated match to rotate through Generator, Totem, vault and Locker grabs.

Silent ApproachQuick DrawTaste the DarknessFrom the Deep

How to do it

  1. Do Generator grabs for Silent Approach.
  2. Do Totem grabs for Quick Draw.
  3. Do vault grabs for Taste the Darkness.
  4. Do Locker grabs for From the Deep.
  5. Reset the interaction and repeat each one as needed.

Notes

  • This is mainly useful because coordinated Survivors can keep presenting the exact grab interaction you need.
