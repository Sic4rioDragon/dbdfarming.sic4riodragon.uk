Healing achievement farm

Keep multiple Survivors injured and repeatedly heal the Obsession while Reactive Healing speeds up every reset.

Healthy ObsessionResurgenceMedicSecond Act

Killer perks

Nemesis (easy Obsession setup)

Survivor perks / setup

Reactive Healing on EVERY SurvivorBoon: Circle of HealingBotany KnowledgeLeader on one Survivor

How to do it

  1. Use Nemesis to make the achievement target the Obsession with a pallet stun.
  2. Keep at least three Survivors injured/downed/hooked for Resurgence progress.
  3. Repeatedly injure and heal the Obsession for Healthy Obsession and Medic.
  4. For Second Act, down the achievement hunter and have someone recover them from Dying State before resetting.

Notes

  • Everyone having Reactive Healing is the base of the healing build. Only one Survivor needs Leader.
