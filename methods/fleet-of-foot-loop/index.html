Survivor

Fleet of Foot speed loop

Babysitter is the clean recommendation; Dark Theory also works but can track inconsistently.

Fleet of Foot

Survivor perks / setup

BabysitterBoon: Dark TheoryShoulder the Burden (optional)

How to do it

  1. With Babysitter, rotate safe unhooks to give the Haste effect.
  2. With Dark Theory, have Survivors walk out of the boon and only re-enter once the previous speed boost is fully gone.
  3. Repeat until the achievement finishes.

Notes

  • Dark Theory works, but you may end up needing far more activations than the listed 50. Shoulder the Burden can give the achievement hunter another useful unhook.
