Dream Demon shack loop

Use the shack gen and fake-pallet wake/sleep loop to keep pulling Survivors back into the Dream World.

Dream Demon

Survivor perks / setup

Deadline

How to do it

  1. Go to the shack Generator and set the four Survivors up around it.
  2. The Survivor closest to the pallet can stay seated while the others move toward them to get pulled into the Dream World.
  3. Once Survivors are out of the Dream World, The Nightmare keeps spawning fake pallets and triggering/exploding them so the loop can start again.
  4. Repeat until the achievement is done.
