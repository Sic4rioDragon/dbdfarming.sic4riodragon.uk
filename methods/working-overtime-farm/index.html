Working Overtime slow-gen farm

This is one of the rare farms where you deliberately keep Generators alive instead of finishing quickly.

Working Overtime

How to do it

  1. Designate one Survivor as the achievement hunter.
  2. Do not give them repair-speed perks or a toolbox.
  3. Let them spend most of the match repairing while the Killer repeatedly damages different Generators.
  4. Once they have enough repair time, finish the match normally.

Notes

  • Normal organised farming can be too fast for this one, so the group has to deliberately slow the Generator phase down.
