Triage healing station

The achievement hunter stays put while the injured Survivor travels back to them for each heal.

Triage

Survivor perks / setup

Reactive Healing on EVERY SurvivorBoon: Circle of HealingBotany KnowledgeLeader on one SurvivorEmpathic Connection (optional)

How to do it

  1. Set up a healing station.
  2. The achievement hunter stays in that small area.
  3. A partner goes more than 16m away and gets hit.
  4. The injured partner comes back to the achievement hunter.
  5. Heal them without the achievement hunter travelling far.
  6. Repeat.

Notes

  • The injured person should come to the healer, not the other way around.
