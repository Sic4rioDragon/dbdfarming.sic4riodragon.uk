Wraith Surprise Attack farm

Alternate uncloaking hits between Survivors while the team resets the damage quickly.

Jump Scares

Survivor perks / setup

Reactive Healing on EVERY SurvivorBoon: Circle of HealingBotany KnowledgeLeader on one Survivor

How to do it

  1. Set up a healing area.
  2. Wraith cloaks, uncloaks directly beside a Survivor and lands the Surprise Attack.
  3. The team heals that Survivor while Wraith recloaks.
  4. Rotate targets and repeat.

Notes

  • Uncloaking-speed add-ons can make the loop less boring.
