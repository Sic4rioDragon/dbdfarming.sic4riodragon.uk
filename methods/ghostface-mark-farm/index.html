Ghost Face Mark farm

Mark, down, reset the Survivor, then repeat without anyone revealing Ghost Face.

Totally Vulnerable

Survivor perks / setup

Reactive Healing on EVERY SurvivorBoon: Circle of HealingBotany KnowledgeLeader on one Survivor

How to do it

  1. Pick one Survivor with a clean sightline.
  2. Ghost Face stalks them until Marked.
  3. Down them.
  4. The other Survivors pick/heal them back up.
  5. Rotate targets and repeat.

Notes

  • Nobody should reveal Ghost Face on purpose during the loop.
