Killer + Survivor

Judgment achievement round

One organised Judgment session can cover most of the new power interactions.

Witness to HeresyUnder my DominionSacrilegeSacrosanctAbove Judgment

How to do it

  1. Do the Witness to Heresy aura build if the Killer still needs it.
  2. Survivors deliberately taunt The Judgment to gain Heresy.
  3. Down and Exile Heretic Survivors for the Exile-related progress.
  4. Let the Survivor escape Exile without getting hit by Seeds of Punishment for Above Judgment.
  5. Line Survivors up at 16m+ for Under my Dominion Divine Light hits.

Notes

  • Taunting can be done with repeated crouches/gestures close to The Judgment, which makes Heresy easy to coordinate.
