Methods / Carry achievement farm
Killer + Survivor

Carry achievement farm

Keep one Survivor carried while the rest rotate hits and fast heals around the Killer.

BodyguardHeavy BurdenMulti-taskerCutting Out

Killer perks

Agitation (recommended)

Survivor perks / setup

Reactive Healing on EVERY SurvivorMettle of Man on Survivors taking repeated Protection HitsBoon: Circle of Healing on one SurvivorLeader on one SurvivorBotany Knowledge / other healing speed perks spread around the team

How to do it

  1. Down Survivor A and pick them up.
  2. Survivors B, C and D rotate taking protection hits while A is carried.
  3. Everyone should have Reactive Healing so nearby hits help the whole reset loop.
  4. Use Circle of Healing, Botany Knowledge and one Leader to heal everyone back up quickly.
  5. For Multi-tasker, let an already injured Survivor get downed while A is still being carried.
  6. When Cutting Out is needed, stop the hit loop and let A wiggle free.
  7. Reset and repeat.

Notes

  • Only one Survivor needs Leader. Agitation is more useful here than Mad Grit because everyone is cooperating and it helps reposition the carry faster.
  • Mettle of Man is very useful for the protection-hit rotation: after the third Protection Hit it shields the next hit that would put that Survivor into the Dying State, letting the loop squeeze in another hit before a reset.
