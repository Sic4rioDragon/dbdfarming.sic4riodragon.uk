Killer Adept farm

Use only the Killer's three unique perks and finish with a fast 4K.

Killer Adepts

How to do it

  1. Bring only that Killer's three Unique Perks.
  2. Do any other power achievement you want first if it does not require extra Killer perks.
  3. For a fast finish, have all four Survivors die in basement.

Notes

  • Add-ons are fine; the Adept restriction is the Killer perk loadout.
