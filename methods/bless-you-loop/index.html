Bless You pallet / Hex loop

Force the same remaining Totem to become Hex: Two Can Play over and over, bless it, snuff it and reset the pallet.

Bless YouHero of Wallachia

Killer perks

Hex: Two Can Play

Survivor perks / setup

Any boon perkAny Means Necessary

How to do it

  1. Cleanse every Totem except one dull Totem next to a pallet.
  2. Stun the Killer once.
  3. The Bless You player starts blessing the remaining Totem.
  4. At about 80% blessing progress, stun the Killer again so Two Can Play activates with almost no wasted time.
  5. Finish the blessing so it counts as blessing a Hex.
  6. The Killer snuffs the boon.
  7. Reset the pallet with Any Means Necessary.
  8. Repeat.

Notes

  • The snuff step matters. If the Killer does not snuff the boon, the loop does not reset cleanly.

Options / variants

Hero of Wallachia stack
If the achievement hunter holds the right item while doing the pallet stuns, this loop can also be used for Hero of Wallachia.
