Witness to Heresy farm

There are a few very easy ways to hold the Obsession's aura long enough to finish the full 300 seconds.

Witness to Heresy

How to do it

  1. Pick whichever option below is easiest for the group.
  2. Keep the Obsession's aura visible until the achievement reaches 300 seconds.

Notes

  • Mettle of Man also makes the Obsession more likely to start on one of the Survivors using it.
  • Mettle of Man currently activates after the third Protection Hit. After healing back to full, the Survivor's aura is revealed to the Killer while they are farther than 12/14/16 metres away depending on perk tier.

Options / variants

Mettle of Man — easiest organized option
Have the Survivors bring Mettle of Man so whoever becomes the Obsession can use it. Trigger Mettle of Man with 3 Protection Hits, heal the Obsession back to full health, then keep them more than 16 metres from the Killer. Their aura stays revealed while the condition holds, so you can basically wait out the 300 seconds.
Perks: Mettle of Man on the Survivors
  1. Get 3 Protection Hits on the Obsession to activate Mettle of Man.
  2. Heal the Obsession back to full health.
  3. Move the Obsession more than 16 metres away from the Killer.
  4. Do not down the Obsession; just keep the aura visible until 300 seconds is finished.
Scratched Mirror Shape
Play The Shape with Scratched Mirror. Keep the Obsession within 32 metres and hold Stalk so their aura stays visible. Since Witness to Heresy itself is not restricted to The Judgment, this can finish the achievement without using the Judgment perk build.
Add-ons: Scratched Mirror
  1. Find the Obsession.
  2. Keep them within 32 metres of The Shape.
  3. Hold Stalk to continuously reveal their aura.
  4. Keep going until the achievement reaches 300 seconds.
The Judgment perk build
The original method still works if you want to do it while playing The Judgment: Celestial Witness, Lethal Pursuer, Friends 'Til the End and Nemesis. It is more active and slower than simply holding a continuous aura read, but it does not need Scratched Mirror.
Perks: Celestial Witness · Lethal Pursuer · Friends 'Til the End · Nemesis
