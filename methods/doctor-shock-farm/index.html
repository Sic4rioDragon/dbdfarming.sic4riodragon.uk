Doctor shock farm

Group all four Survivors and hit as many of them as possible with every Shock Therapy.

Mad HouseShocking Treatment

Killer add-ons

Discipline add-ons (recommended for the faster Shock Therapy detonation)

How to do it

  1. Have all four Survivors stand tightly grouped.
  2. Shock the whole group until everybody reaches Madness Tier III for Mad House.
  3. Keep them grouped and continue hitting as many Survivors as possible per Shock Therapy.
  4. Have Survivors Snap Out of It when needed and keep repeating.

Notes

  • The point is to make every Shock Therapy hit multiple people instead of grinding 500 one at a time.
