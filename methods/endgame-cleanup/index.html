Survivor perks / setup

Reactive Healing on EVERY Survivor

How to do it

  1. Finish the normal farm and power the Exit Gates.
  2. Open a Gate when you are ready to start Endgame Collapse.
  3. Do post-Generator heals for Ever Vigilant.
  4. Take protection hits during Endgame Collapse for Final Days.
  5. Hook and rescue someone after the Gates are powered for No One Left Behind.
  6. Achievement hunters for Break For It leave while Broken.
  7. Rotate who opens Gates for Escaping the Nightmare.
