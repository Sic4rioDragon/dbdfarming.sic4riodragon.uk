What this site is for

This is here to explain the main farming styles in DBD and point people toward the actual resources, methods and groups behind them.

The main thing I recommend is organized farming. If everybody in the lobby knows what is going on and is okay with it, it is the cleanest and most efficient way to do it.

Recommended

Organized farming

Full group, everyone knows the plan, faster methods and much less chance of the match going wrong.

Read more →

Asian farming

Easier to get into alone and usually relaxed, but slower and less reliable because the lobby is not organized ahead of time.

Bloodpoints & XP

A proper farming round is usually around 10 minutes and, if done right, you normally max everything including XP.

How do I find a group?

The easiest way is the Bloodpoint Farming Discord, the biggest and most active farming community for this. Outside of events it can be quieter, but anyone can make a group and there is usually still at least one group around on normal days.
