What this is

This is here to explain the farming styles I actually use in DBD and keep the useful methods in one place. The main thing I recommend is organized farming: everybody knows the plan, everybody agrees to it, and the group can use much faster methods without relying on random players understanding what is happening.

Recommended

Organized farming

Full group, VC, known methods and everyone agrees to the farm before the match. This is the best option if you can get the group together.

Asian farming

Easy to solo queue into when it works and usually very relaxed, but slower and less reliable because the lobby is not organized ahead of time.

How do I find a group?

The easiest place is the Bloodpoint Farming Discord. It is the biggest and most active farming community for this. Outside of events it can be quieter, but anyone can make a group and there is usually still at least one group around on normal days.
